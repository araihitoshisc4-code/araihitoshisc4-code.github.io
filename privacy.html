Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Purpose

Hitoshi Lab rclone is a personal application used by its owner to synchronize selected Google Drive files with a local computer using rclone.

Data Access and Use

The application accesses Google Drive data only for synchronization and local personal analysis. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Data Storage and Sharing

Synchronized data is stored on the owner's local computer. The application does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers.

Security

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally and are not published on this website.

Contact

For questions about this application, use the developer contact information provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
